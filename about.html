WHO WE ARE

Rama Homes Ltd is a fully registered dynamic real estate company actively involved in the identification and development of off plan property. We conduct feasibility analysis to determine the viability of each specific project and negotiate the acquisition of profitable projects on behalf of our clients/partners.

OUR MISSION

Our mission is to re-define the property development business standards through impartiality and honesty. To make it easy for our customers to become home owners with our flexible payment plans at zero percent interest rates.

Our vision

Our vision is to succeed, enabling our diverse team of valued employees to hold great accountability towards our esteemed customers. We aim high, with integrity, diversification, and consistency.

Our Philosophy

Our philosophy is that success should be shared and will be enhanced with team based approach characterized by mutual understanding, enthusiasm and drive. This result orientated approach ensures our clients are treated as partners and enables both the business and service targets we set together to be achieved.
