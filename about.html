WHO WE ARE

At Zentro Holdings Limited, we are a purpose-driven organization dedicated to delivering impactful solutions in Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH), project development, and community empowerment. Based in Kenya, we work to enhance the well-being and dignity of individuals and communities through innovative, sustainable, and tailored approaches. Our expertise spans across providing critical WASH services, developing dignity and hygiene kits for vulnerable populations, crafting competitive project proposals, conducting feasibility studies, and delivering customized training programs. We partner with NGOs, government bodies, and private enterprises to create meaningful change and drive social and economic growth. Guided by our core values of integrity, innovation, sustainability, collaboration, and a strong community focus, we are committed to empowering marginalized groups, fostering partnerships, and delivering high-quality products and services that inspire trust and promote inclusive progress. Zentro Holdings Limited — Empowering Communities, Driving Change.

OUR MISSION

To deliver high-quality products and services that enhance health, dignity, and opportunities for growth while fostering strong partnerships.

Our vision
